1. Executive Summary & Core Quantitative Benchmarks

An exploratory data analysis (EDA) was performed on a multi-variable commercial intelligence dataset containing 1,500 transactions across 8 metropolitan commercial hubs, spanning from January 2025 to March 2026. The dataset captures both Gross Invoiced Order Amount ($9,447,405 USD) and Final Net Cash Collected ($8,345,376 USD) after discounting, representing purchases across five strategic technology product categories and three customer segments (Enterprise, Small Business, Consumer).

2. Source of Truth & Discrepancy Resolution

Statistical verification directly on the source dataset (ecommerce_customer_intelligence.csv) establishes the exact parametric and non-parametric parameters for Net Revenue:

  • First Quartile (\(Q_1\)): $1,066.80 USD
  • Third Quartile (\(Q_3\)): $5,570.18 USD
  • Interquartile Range (\(IQR\)): $5,570.18 - $1,066.80 = $4,503.38 USD
  • Tukey Upper Fence Calculation: \[Q_3 + 1.5 \times IQR = \$5,570.18 + (1.5 \times \$4,503.38) = \mathbf{\$12,325.24\text{ USD}}\]
  • Affected Outliers: Exactly 119 transactions (7.93% of the dataset) exceed $12,325.24.

Resolution: The earlier transcription typo listing \(Q_3\) as $5,688.38 has been reconciled against the CSV source of truth. All calculations, UI filters, inspectors, and tests now align on $12,325.24 and 119 matching records.

3. Key Business Insights & Statistical Associations

Finding 1: Discounting Tipping Point and Margin Destruction

Pearson correlation reveals a moderate inverse statistical association (\(r = -0.58\)) between discount percentages and realized margins. Discounts between 0% and 12% yield healthy 29.4% to 36.5% margins. When discounts exceed 30%, transactions trigger severe negative margins (-23.77% average operating loss).

Finding 2: Enterprise Dominance & Average Order Value (AOV) vs LTV

The Enterprise customer segment accounts for 53.19% of total gross order volume ($4,439,221 USD) across only 223 transactions (14.87% of order count). The Enterprise Average Order Value is $19,906.82, which is 8.57x higher than Consumer ($2,322.01). Note: Single-order AOV is analyzed here; lifetime value (LTV) requires tracking repeat multi-year contracts.

Finding 3: Logistics Transit Duration and Churn Risk Score

Delivery time correlates strongly with customer ratings (\(r = -0.71\)) and predicted churn risk score (\(r = +0.64\)). Deliveries completed within 3 days achieve 4.59 stars and 0.18 churn risk score. Delays exceeding 7 days collapse ratings to 2.57 stars and elevate churn risk score to 0.58.

5. Methodology, Provenance, & Analytical Limitations

  • Provenance: Source dataset data/ecommerce_customer_intelligence.csv verified with zero missing values across 1,500 records.
  • Parametric Metrics: Computed using Python 3.14 SciPy & Pandas standard libraries.
  • Non-Parametric Metrics: Quartiles computed via linear interpolation; Tukey fences computed as \([Q_1 - 1.5 \times IQR, Q_3 + 1.5 \times IQR]\).
  • Limitations: Churn Risk Score is a model-derived predictive propensity metric (0.00 to 1.00), not an observed contractual churn event. Observed relationships denote statistical associations rather than controlled experimental trials.
